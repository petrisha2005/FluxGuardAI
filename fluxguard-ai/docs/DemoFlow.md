# FluxGuard AI — Interactive Recruiter & Reviewer Demo Flow Guide

Welcome to the **FluxGuard AI Operations Suite** interactive demo guide. This walkthrough is optimized for recruiters, hiring managers, and judges who want to experience the platform's full command capabilities immediately.

---

## Getting Started

1. **Start the Platform**: Open the application in your browser.
2. **Access Mission Control**: Log in with default credentials or bypass via authentication to reach the primary Operations Dashboard.
3. **Activate Demo Mode**: Locate the **Demo Mode** switch in the top header and toggle it **ON**. A flashing warning banner will appear: `⚠️ DEMO MODE ACTIVE · SIMULATION DATA OVERLAY`.

---

## Interactive Walkthrough Steps

### Phase 1: Real-Time Egress Analytics & Twin Overview
- **Action**: Open the **Digital Twin** tab on the dashboard or navigate to the Map view via the Quick Navigation buttons at the bottom.
- **Telemetry Indicators**: Notice the high crowd density stress triggers at **Gate C** (breaching 86% of structural limits). In simulated mode, safety posture alerts fire continuously to demonstrate threshold management heuristics.

### Phase 2: Command Palette Portal (Ctrl + K)
- **Action**: Press `Ctrl + K` (or `Cmd + K` on Mac) anywhere on the platform.
- **Search Query**: Type `Gate C` or `Redirect`. 
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Use the arrow keys to navigate down to **AI Decision #201: Redirect 500 attendees from Gate C** or **Incident #101: Gate C Crowd Surge**, and press `Enter` to jump straight to the respective control screens.

### Phase 3: Human-in-the-Loop AI Approvals
- **Action**: Navigate to the **Autonomous AI** command console.
- **Decision Deck**: Find the pending recommendation cards. Review the **Rationale**, **Confidence Index (94%)**, and **Expected Outcomes**.
- **Clearance Approval**: Fill in your **Operator Signature** (e.g. `Lead-Reviewer`), and click **Approve & Execute**. The decision card will transition to **✓ Approved** and update the recent execution history logs.

### Phase 4: Emergency Management Dispatch
- **Action**: On the Dashboard or Incidents feed, locate the active warning log.
- **Responder Dispatch**: Click on **Dispatch Responder**, assign a security unit, and witness the incident transition from **Pending** to **Dispatched**, and then resolve the incident cleanly to stabilize venue health.
