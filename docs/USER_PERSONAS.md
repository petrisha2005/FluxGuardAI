# User Personas

## Fan

Name: Maya Rodriguez  
Context: International visitor attending a World Cup match with friends.  
Primary device: Mobile phone.  
Goals:

- Enter the stadium with minimal confusion.
- Avoid unsafe crowding and long queues.
- Find gates, concessions, restrooms, transport, and exits.
- Receive guidance in clear, non-technical language.

Pain points:

- Unfamiliar venue layout.
- Language barriers.
- Anxiety when routes are blocked or crowds surge.
- Distrust of generic alerts that do not explain what to do.

Needs:

- Simple route recommendations.
- Time-sensitive alerts.
- Accessible instructions.
- Confidence that guidance is official and safe.

## Volunteer

Name: Liam Chen  
Context: Assigned to a gate and concourse section during peak arrival.  
Primary device: Mobile or tablet.  
Goals:

- Understand what action to take now.
- Direct fans clearly.
- Escalate issues quickly.
- Avoid conflicting instructions from different teams.

Pain points:

- Limited situational awareness outside assigned zone.
- High noise and pressure.
- Unclear escalation paths.
- Instructions that are too broad to apply locally.

Needs:

- Role-specific tasks.
- Zone-specific recommendations.
- Escalation contacts.
- Short, confident language.

## Stadium Operator

Name: Aisha Patel  
Context: Works in the venue operations center monitoring live crowd flow.  
Primary device: Desktop command dashboard.  
Goals:

- Identify risk before it becomes an incident.
- Coordinate staff, gates, signage, and announcements.
- Track alerts and decisions.
- Maintain safety and throughput.

Pain points:

- Too many feeds and not enough synthesis.
- False alarms.
- Delayed field confirmation.
- Pressure to make rapid decisions with incomplete information.

Needs:

- Forecasts with confidence and drivers.
- Severity-ranked risk list.
- Operational playbooks.
- Audit trail of decisions and outcomes.

## Event Organizer

Name: Sofia Martinez  
Context: Responsible for event-wide safety, experience, and post-event reporting.  
Primary device: Desktop analytics view.  
Goals:

- Monitor event performance across venues and time windows.
- Understand operational bottlenecks.
- Improve plans for future matches.
- Demonstrate safety and experience outcomes to stakeholders.

Pain points:

- Inconsistent reporting.
- Data scattered across teams and vendors.
- Difficulty proving intervention impact.
- Limited historical comparisons.

Needs:

- Summary dashboards.
- Exportable reports.
- Trend analysis.
- Evidence of forecast accuracy and response effectiveness.
